🎢 Rollercoaster / 🔀 Swing after Swing (0-3 pts)

A side "leads" at ±1.5 pawns. A blip is a lead that's gone again after the opponent's very next move (one player blunders, the other gives it straight back). A game gets one of the two, never both.

ConditionPoints
Rollercoaster: the lead changed hands 3+ times, blips not counted+3
Swing after swing: 3+ lead changes only when blips are counted (not a Rollercoaster)+2

Time Pressure (requires clocks + Lichess analysis)

A player is in time trouble when they have under 90 seconds for 3+ of their moves, in a game that was still open when it started: level within 1.5 pawns, with 4+ queens, rooks, bishops or knights on the board. One chip per game at most.
