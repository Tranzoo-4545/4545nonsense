Alternates vs Games-Played Share

Alternate share = (distinct players − teams×boards) / players, a proxy for how much of the season ran on alternates. Played share = pairings with an actual game link. Bright dots = last 15 seasons.

Verdict: Pearson r = +0.103 across all seasons (+0.166 for the last 15) — essentially no relationship. Slope: each additional percentage point of alternate share moves the played share by +0.06 pp.

Caveat: both measures drift over the league's history for independent reasons, so the all-seasons correlation partly reflects era, not causation — the recent-seasons r is the fairer test. The alternate count is a proxy: unfilled slots or mid-season roster changes blur it slightly.
