📊 Hype Score Calculation

Each pairing is scored based on several factors that make a matchup compelling:

👑 Titled Player (0-3 pts)

ConditionPoints
One titled player (FM, IM, GM, etc.)+1.5
Both players titled+3

🎯 Milestone Games (0-6 pts)

ConditionPoints
Player's 45th career game+2
Player's 100th career game+3
Player's 200th/300th career game+3

Can stack if both players have milestones

🏆 100th Win (0-2.5 pts)

ConditionPoints
Player at 99 wins (going for 100th)+2.5

⚔️ Head-to-Head History (0-4 pts)

ConditionPoints
Played once before+2
Played twice before+3
Played 3+ times (Rivals!)+4

🐺 Met in LoneWolf / Chess960 (0-1.5 pts)

ConditionPoints
Played each other in LoneWolf or Chess960+1
...and the last meeting was within the past 4 weeks+1.5

Counted once, even if they met in both leagues

🔥 Form Clash (0-3 pts)

ConditionPoints
Both on 2+ win streak+2
Both on 3+ win streak+3
Both on 2+ loss streak+2
Both on 3+ loss streak+3

Streaks are career-wide, not just current season

🏆 Team Standings Impact (0-6 pts)

ConditionBase Points
#1 team matchup (by avg placement)+4
#2 team matchup+3
#3 team matchup+2

Round multiplier: R1: 0x, R2: 0.25x, R3: 0.5x, R4: 0.75x, R5-6: 1x, R7-8: 1.5x

🎖️ Veteran Experience (0-4 pts)

ConditionPoints
Career games / 100 (per player, max 2 each)0-4

e.g., 150 games = 1.5 pts, 200+ games = 2 pts (capped)

🪃 Return Match (0-4 pts)

ConditionPoints
First game back after 5-9 seasons+2
First game back after 10-19 seasons+3
First game back after 20+ seasons+4

🤝 Former Teammates (0-5 pts)

ConditionPoints
Teammates on 1 shared team (both 4+ games for it, or both on its final roster)+2
Teammates on 2 shared teams+3
Teammates on 3+ shared teams+4
Bonus: Won league together+1

👀 Vets First Meeting (0-1.5 pts)

ConditionPoints
Both 80+ games, never faced each other+0.75
Both 100+ games, never faced each other+1.5

Not if they have met in LoneWolf or Chess960
