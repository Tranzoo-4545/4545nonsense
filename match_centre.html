How we predict a game

Every prediction starts from the two players’ Lichess ratings. White gets a small edge for moving first.

Then we look at each player’s league history in three ways:

  • League record: if a player has scored more (or less) in the league than their ratings suggested, we count part of that. A few games barely count; it takes a long record to make a real difference.
  • Usual level: if a player’s rating is currently below where it has been in their league games, we count part of that back, since dips are often temporary. The same goes the other way.
  • Draw habit: some players draw much more or much less than average, so their games get a higher or lower draw chance.

A new player is predicted from their rating alone, and their history counts more as their games add up.
